# 🐸 nUndertale — Game Script & Game Design Document (GDD)

> **Production Note:** An independent, solo indie passion project. A satirical comedic parody tribute to Toby Fox's *Undertale*, celebrating retro RPG conventions, chiptune aesthetic, and classic internet meme culture.
>
> **Canonical lore:** [`docs/LORE.md`](docs/LORE.md) is the source of truth for the world, cast, structure (~25–30 min), routes, endings and art/audio direction. Where this script conflicts with it, `docs/LORE.md` wins.

## 1. Overview & Project Philosophy

*nUndertale* subverts traditional RPG tropes. Instead of an innocent human child stumbling into an underground kingdom of misunderstood monsters seeking peace, we have **Pepe**, a cynical, sleep-deprived, chronically online gamer who falls down the forgotten chasm of **Mt. Dew**.

Deep below, the underworld is not populated by storybook creatures, but by exiled internet factions: **Furries in elaborate fursuits**, **Weeaboos obsessed with dakimakuras (body pillows)**, and **over-enthusiastic roleplayers**.

### The Core Subversion: The Curse of Furrification
Unlike *Undertale*, where defeat simply shatters your SOUL into pieces, *nUndertale* introduces a far more humiliating fate:
- When Pepe's HP hits 0, he does not physically perish. Instead, he **absorbs the surrounding cringe**, triggering a gradual physical metamorphosis.
- With each careless hit, Pepe grows fuzzy ears, soft paws, a sparkly tail, and eventually becomes the official mascot of the local convention.
- Pepe's objective is straightforward: **recover his wired mechanical keyboard, navigate the perilous halls of Mt. Dew, and make it back to his bedroom before his raid group kicks him from the Discord server.**

## 2. World Lore & History

### The Era of the Great Flame Wars
Many firmware cycles ago, two factions ruled supreme over the web: **GAMERS** and **FURRIES/WEEABOOS**.
The conflict was not fought with steel or cannons, but through relentless forum raids, copypastas, and heated Discord voice calls.

Eventually, the Gamers triumphed through pure toxicity and low-latency dial-up connections, banishing the Furries and Weeaboos into the subterranean depths of **Mt. Dew** — a desolate chasm sealed by a magical barrier of expired deodorant and nonexistent Wi-Fi signals.

Legend whispered throughout the eons:
> *"Those who dare ascend Mt. Dew shall never return the same; they will either plunge into endless fursuit conventions, or wander forever entangled in body pillow harems."*

## 3. Major Characters

### 1. Pepe the Fallen Gamer (Protagonist)
- **Appearance:** The classic Pepe meme frog (heavy-lidded eyes, wide frowning lips) with dark eye circles from 48-hour MMO marathons, sporting a nacho-stained blue T-shirt and flip-flops.
- **Personality:** Sarcastic, unmotivated, pragmatically blunt, yet holding an unwavering hatred for cringe.
- **Starting Weapon:** Frayed USB Optical Mouse.
- **Starting Armor:** One-Sided Broken Gaming Headset.

### 2. Furiell (Guardian of the Ruins / Toriel Parody)
- **Appearance:** A motherly, fluffy fursuiter wearing half-moon reading glasses with a glaringly visible zipper running down her back.
- **Personality:** Overwhelmingly caring, smotheringly protective, and determined to make every newcomer feel "safe in the fandom."
- **Habits:** Bakes glitter-covered pop-tarts, offers warm blankets made of questionable faux-fur, and constantly checks Pepe's temperature to see if his gamer brain is overheated.
- **Conflict:** Refuses to let Pepe leave the Ruins because the outside world is filled with "toxic trolls and people who don't understand our artistic self-expression."

### 3. Senpai Sans (The Weeaboo Skeleton)
- **Appearance:** A short, lazy skeleton wearing a blue hoodie, pink slippers, and a ninja headband from the Hidden Leaf Village. Never parts with his waifu dakimakura.
- **Personality:** Chronic fourth-wall breaker, excessively laid-back, speaks in broken anime catchphrases and painful puns (*"heh, i guess you could say my jokes are... bare bones-desu, ne?"*).
- **Role:** Observes Pepe's gamer status across save points and delivers judgmental comedic monologues.

### 4. Papyrus the Aspiring Streamer
- **Appearance:** Tall, flamboyant skeleton wearing cardboard armor crafted from graphics card boxes, held together by duct tape and flickering RGB light strips.
- **Personality:** Enthusiastic, loud, dreams of reaching 10 concurrent viewers and being recognized as a "Verified Partner of the Underground."
- **Puzzles:** Constructs absurd trials throughout the underground, such as laser grids made of disco lights, mandatory CAPTCHA floor tiles, and speedtyping tests.

### 5. Barky the Alpha Wolf (Ruins Boss / Mini-boss)
- **Appearance:** An edgy teenager wearing faux wolf ears, a studded pleather jacket, plastic chains, and fingerless gloves.
- **Behavior:** Howls dramatically at fluorescent ceiling lights and claims to lead the "Shadow Fang Pack," but gets completely disoriented and pacified if you shine a red laser pointer or offer Mountain Dew.

## 4. Battle System Mechanics & Parody Design

The combat system replicates Undertale's iconic split-screen battle box with satirical meme bullet-hell dodging:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------+
|                      [ BARKY ]                        |
|                                                       |
|       "OwO what's this? A hairless intruder?!"        |
+-------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                       |
|             +---------------------------+             |
|             |          [   ]            |             |
|             |          [ <3]            |             |
|             |                           |             |
|             +---------------------------+             |
|                                                       |
|  PEPE   LV 1   HP 20 / 20    FURRY-METER: [          ] |
+-------------------------------------------------------+
|  [ FIGHT ]      [ ACT ]      [ ITEM ]      [ MERCY ]  |
+-------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Action Commands:
1. **[ FIGHT ]:**
   - Initiates an elliptical timing gauge with a sweeping white reticle.
   - Hitting the center deals critical "Gamer Damage" with a classic FPS hitmarker sound effect.
2. **[ ACT ]:**
   - **Check:** Displays enemy stats, attack patterns, and cringe weaknesses.
   - **Cringe Check:** Points out the visible zipper on the opponent's fursuit, causing immense embarrassment.
   - **Offer Mountain Dew:** Bribes the opponent with vintage caffeinated soda, turning their name yellow for immediate sparing.
   - **Debate Anime Lore:** Recites incoherent timeline lore from 90s mecha anime, leaving the enemy bewildered.
3. **[ ITEM ]:**
   - *Mountain Dew (Warm):* Restores 10 HP and grants temporary movement speed.
   - *Stale Doritos:* Restores 6 HP.
   - *Duct Tape:* Repairs 15% dignity (reduces the Furry-Meter).
4. **[ MERCY ]:**
   - **Spare:** Peacefully resolves the conflict when the enemy's name is highlighted in yellow.
   - **Flee:** Pepe runs away backwards with comic dust clouds.

## 5. Chapter 1 Scene Script: "The Descent into Mt. Dew"

### Scene 1: The Fall and The Spark of Determination
- **Setting:** A dark cavern clearing floored with cracked purple tiles littered with crumpled chip bags and empty neon-green cans. A solitary ray of light shines down from the ceiling chasm.
- **Music:** Soft ambient drone transitioning into a melancholic 8-bit chiptune melody (*Once Upon A Time Remix*).
- **Action:** Pepe slowly stands up, rubs his sore amphibian forehead, and grunts:
  > **Pepe:** *"Ugh... where's my wireless mouse? And why does it smell like Axe Body Spray and cotton candy down here...?"*
- **Interactive Save Point:**
  > *(A brilliant 4-pointed golden star sparkles atop a vintage soda bottle).*
  > *"The scent of lukewarm energy drinks and stale Doritos fills you with DETERMINATION."*
  > *[ File saved. HP fully restored. ]*

### Scene 2: The Ruins Hallway & Ancient Signs
- **Setting:** Stone arches draped in vintage banner ribbons reading *"Midwest FurFest 2014 - Welcome!"*
- **Notice Board on Wall:**
  > *"NOTICE FROM RUINS ADMINISTRATION: Please refrain from shedding faux-fur in the ventilation shafts. Violators will be banned from the Minecraft server."*
- **Ancient CRT Computer in the Corner:**
  > *"An ancient CRT monitor humming quietly. It's displaying a blue screen of death with an error message in Comic Sans."*

### Scene 3: The First Encounter — Barky the Alpha Wolf
- As Pepe approaches the northern archway, dramatic dramatic howling echoes off the stonework.
- Barky drops down into a crouch, crossing his arms edgily:
  > **Barky:** *"Halt, hairless intruder! *[awkward howling]* You have stepped into the dark domain of the Shadow Fang Pack! I am Barky, the Alpha Wolf of Mt. Dew! Prepare to face my fursona!"*
- **Combat Commences:**
  - Turn 1: Barky throws cascading pink "UWU" projectile letters and bouncing Mountain Dew cans.
  - Pepe chooses `ACT -> Cringe Check`: Pepe stares directly at the zipper on Barky's back.
  - Barky blushes through his wolf mask: *"H-hey! Stop looking at the zipper! It's an artisanal design feature, b-baka!"*
  - Pepe chooses `ACT -> Offer Mountain Dew`: Pepe cracks open an ice-cold can.
  - Barky's name turns bright yellow: *"Is that... citrus blast flavor?! Okay, maybe you aren't so bad after all!"*
  - Pepe chooses `MERCY -> Spare`: Victory fanfare plays! 10 Gold acquired!

### Scene 4: The Path to Snowdinha
- With Barky befriended, the massive iron gate swings open, revealing a frosty pine forest under neon aurora lights.
- In the distance, a figure in a blue hoodie and ninja headband leans lazily against a wooden sentry station, watching Pepe's every step.

## 7. C++20 Clean SOLID Architecture

1. **Single Responsibility Principle (SRP):**
   - Independent modules: `AudioService`, `ResourceService`, `InputService`, `SceneManager`, `Player`, `DialogBox`, `BattleSystem`.
2. **Open/Closed Principle (OCP):**
   - Extensible through `IScene` and `IInteractable` interfaces. New maps, minigames, and puzzles plug in seamlessly.
3. **Liskov Substitution Principle (LSP):**
   - All scenes and entities share uniform polymorphic lifecycles without typecasting hacks.
4. **Interface Segregation Principle (ISP):**
   - Clean, lightweight abstract contracts (`IScene`, `IAudioService`, `IResourceService`, `IInputService`).
5. **Dependency Inversion Principle (DIP):**
   - Centralized `GameContext` injects service interfaces into components, completely eradicating brittle procedural global state and singletons.
