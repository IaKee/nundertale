## 1. Executive Summary

The round 7 opening overhaul successfully introduces the Landing (R1), the quiet badge pickup and panel hall (R1a/R1b), Floofy's boss lair with the two-variant lethal encounter and Furiell's spray-bottle rescue (R2), and the v3.3 goblin-myth dialogue.

However, an exhaustive line-by-line audit against `ref/LORE.md` v3.2/v3.3 reveals **several discrepancies, stale strings, and one critical content-rule violation**:
1. **Critical content-rule violation (§2.2 / v3.3 "my child" forbidden):** In `data/maps/ruins_dummy.json` (and `tools/maps/build_ruins_maps.py:2242`), Furiell addresses the player as `"my child"`, which directly violates LORE §2.2 and contracts.md.
2. **Character voice divergence (§10.2):** In `data/maps/ruins_entrance.json` (and `tools/maps/build_ruins_maps.py:1722`), Furiell's NPC lines use formal, un-uwu'd English (`"cruel people"`, `"make friends"`) instead of her established love-bombing cringe uwu dialogue (`"meanies with cameras"`, `">w<"`, `"fwiends"`, `"hun"`).
3. **Exact copy differences vs ref LORE (§12 & §18.1):**
   - Dummy Talk ACT in `data/enemies/fursuit_dummy.json` uses an unaligned line instead of canonical `* It doesn't seem much for conversation.`
   - Dummy prop description in `data/maps/ruins_dummy.json` says "mannequin wearing a fursuit head" instead of "coat rack wearing a friend's spare fursuit".
   - `data/cutscenes/r1_tide_pod.json` omits the second player thought `* (Forbidden candy.)`.
   - `data/dialogue/en/ruins.json` retains the obsolete v1/v2 `"bed of discarded snack wrappers"` fall line.
   - Missing Grub thought in R4: `* (You've done this a thousand times. In the RGB app.)` is omitted from data.
4. **Obsolete v3.1 cutscenes still in tree:** `r2_floofy.json` and `r2_floofy_rescue.json` contain the obsolete v3.1 glitter rescue (`"My glitter is hypoallergenic. It washes the fluff right off."`) and pre-v3.2 dialogue superseded by `r2_floofy_scene.json` / `r2_floofy_end.json`.
5. **Legacy asset references (D-16 / v3.3):** `ruins_fall.json` and `r1_wake.json` still point to `assets/sprites/ow/pepe_wakeup.png` rather than human Grub NEET assets.
