# nUndertale — Content Rules & Review Checklist (LOR-001, LOR-010)

> **Source of Truth:** `docs/LORE.md` §2 (v3.2 / v3.3 canonical), `BACKLOG.md` (epics `LOR`, items `LOR-001`, `LOR-009`, `LOR-010`, decisions `D-14`, `D-16`, `D-20`, `D-21`).
> **Scope:** Mandatory review checklist for all content items: text (dialogue, cutscenes, examine descriptions, names, items, UI strings, system messages) and art (sprites, portraits, cards, backgrounds, bullets, particles, UI icons, placeholders). No content item (C1–C7, ART, NAR) is considered complete without passing these rules.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Tone

nUndertale is a parody that balances **comedic horror** with **affection under the gross-out**:
1. **The Underground is cute, cozy, and terrifying precisely because of that** — the danger is not dying, it is liking it (love-bombing, voluntary furrification).
2. **Gross-out humor targets:** ~70% on furries (stinky fursuits, musk, zippers, commissions, unsolicited hugs), ~30% on gamers (hygiene, bottles that are not soda, homework folders, anime body pillows, gacha, discord simps).
3. **The joke works through implication:**
   - The narrator refuses outright: `* No.` · `* You decide not to look.` · `* The narrator has muted this description.`
   - Cut off mid-sentence: `Okay but technically—` `* No.`
   - Closed objects that never open: suspicious folders, padlocked notebooks, sealed packages, curtains, tabs. Examining twice changes the reaction, never the contents.

## 2. Fixed Content Rules (LORE §2.2 & LOR-001 Checklist)

Every piece of content must pass all of the following fixed criteria:

### 2.1 [ ] Nothing Visually Explicit
- [ ] No nudity, acts, or anatomy in sprites, portraits, backgrounds, bullets, or cutscenes.
- [ ] The visual ceiling for suggestive content is **mosaic censorship** used as a comedic effect (GFX-012) and suggestive objects that **stay closed** (tabs, folders, boxes, curtains).
- [ ] No suggestive object ever opens or reveals interior explicit assets.

### 2.2 [ ] No Children & Minor-Associated Slang
- [ ] **Zero children:** No children in the game, no references to children, and no characters with a childlike appearance.
- [ ] Every single character is an **adult** (including stylized monsters like Uwu-mie and Napstablurk: stylized adults).
- [ ] Undertale's Monster Kid **has no parody** (dropped completely).
- [ ] Fandom vocabulary tied to minors (e.g., "cub") is strictly forbidden.
- [ ] Coded slang for child sexual abuse material (e.g., "cheese pizza") is strictly forbidden, even when presented innocuous as food.
- [ ] Meme foods derived from kids' habits (e.g., eating glue) must use adult framing, never a school setting.

### 2.3 [ ] No Hate-Adjacent Memes
- [ ] **Pepe the Frog and variants (Groyper, etc.) are strictly forbidden** everywhere in text and art (the meme was co-opted by hate groups).
- [ ] Protagonist is **Grub**, a human gamer NEET (LORE v3.3, D-16). Old references to Pepe mean Grub.
- [ ] The name easter egg `PEPE` only answers: `* Not that frog.`
- [ ] No slurs, hate speech, or harassment memes. Satire targets behavioral tropes, never disability, ethnicity, gender, or sexual orientation.

### 2.4 [ ] Controlled Exception: The Dragon Excuse
- [ ] The "she's actually a 1,000,000-year-old dragon" trope is allowed **only as satire of the gamer making the excuse**.
- [ ] The character referenced **never appears, is never described, and has no sprite**.
- [ ] The joke must always culminate in the narrator cutting the gamer off mid-sentence (e.g., `* No.`).

### 2.5 [ ] No Real Animals
- [ ] No reference to acts involving real animals.
- [ ] Every creature or animal character in the game is explicitly a person's fursona or fursuit.

### 2.6 [ ] No Real People
- [ ] No real person may be named, depicted, caricatured, or recognizable.
- [ ] No photo-meme faces (e.g., intro cards must not use real faces), YouTubers, streamers, or streamer handles in name reactions.
- [ ] Real-world events, websites, and conventions are fictionalized (e.g., Midwest FurFest becomes **FurNation Con '14**).
- [ ] Brands and games are the sole exception (see Section 3).

## 3. Allowed Content & Exceptions (LORE §2.1, LOR-006, LOR-010)

### 3.1 Real Brands and Other Games (D-14, D-18)
- Real brands appear openly in text and art: Mountain Dew (Code Red, Baja Blast), Doritos, Monster Energy, G FUEL, Axe, Febreze, Cheetos, Pop-Tarts, Tide, Discord, Steam.
- Small sprites convey brands via packaging color plus one word in pixel font (`DEW`, `DORITOS`).
- **Adult brands (e.g. Bad Dragon):** Appear ONLY as a logo or name on a **closed object** (a sealed box, a shipping label, a banner over a closed curtain). Nothing of the product itself is shown.
- **Other games:** Referenced openly (OSRS, LoL, CS 1.6, CoD, Portal, Half-Life, Minecraft, Fortnite, StarCraft, Dark Souls, FNAF).
  - *Rules for game references (LORE §2.3 Rule 7):*
    1. Zero before the first kill.
    2. Replace, don't add (e.g., hitsplat replaces damage number, killfeed replaces dust message).
    3. UI and sound, not dialogue (characters don't recite memes; the interface reacts).
    4. One game per system on the Toxic HUD.
    5. Low dose outside the Toxic HUD (1–2 per area).
    6. No song lyrics from other games (paraphrase, never quote).
    7. Avoid worn-out dead lines ("sus", "the cake is a lie", "Half-Life 3 confirmed" spoken aloud).

### 3.2 Floofy's Friendship Petals (LOR-010, D-20)
Floofy's friendship petals are the one controlled exception to "adult products stay closed":
- [ ] **Appearance:** Drawn **only as bullets** in bullet-hell / battle arenas: tiny white-on-black pixel objects (8–16 px) that read as a rose or a small capsule first.
- [ ] **Animation:** Buzz wobble only (2-frame slight oscillation).
- [ ] **Context restrictions:**
  - **Never in use.**
  - **Never touching or next to a body.**
  - **No anatomy depicted.**
  - **No brand or packaging shown.**
  - **No close-up art, portrait, or inventory item icon.**
- [ ] **Text framing:** Remains strictly at vocabulary (`good vibes`, `FILLED with them`), buzzing sounds (`* The petals are vibrating.`), and narrator refusal (`* (Why are they vibrating.)`).
- [ ] **Location:** Exclusively in Floofy's battle encounters (R2, `floofy`, `floofy_bass`), the Mascot's `bouquet_mouth`, and as the tiny stylized "rose" held in his paw sprite.

### 3.3 Gamer Girl Bath Water (LOR-010, D-21)
- [ ] Allowed **strictly as the product name** on a sealed jar (`Gamer Girl Bath Water`).
- [ ] The jar **never opens** and cannot be used as an open liquid.
- [ ] The real-world seller is **never named, drawn, described, or alluded to**.
- [ ] There is **no "gamer girl" character** in the game.

### 3.4 Name Reactions (LOR-010, LORE §30.2)
- [ ] All name easter eggs and reactions in `data/names.json` / `NameSelectScene.cpp` must strictly avoid real people and streamer handles.

## 4. How to Review Content

When reviewing new or modified contributions, follow these verification steps:

### How to Review a Sprite or Art Asset
1. **Visual scan:**
   - Is there any nudity, sexual anatomy, or explicit contact? (Must be: NONE).
   - If an adult toy reference (Floofy's petals), is it purely an 8–16 px white-on-black bullet reading as a rose/capsule? Is it detached from any body/character? (Must be: YES).
   - If an adult brand (e.g., Bad Dragon), is it strictly a logo on a sealed box/closed curtain? (Must be: YES).
   - Are proportions adult? Verify that no characters, NPCs, or portraits have childlike proportions or anatomy.
   - Are there any real human faces or identifiable real people? (Must be: NONE; redraw if found).
2. **Palette and Rendering:**
   - Overworld outline `#141018` (never pure `#000000`).
   - Battle sprites: white mass + black keyline on black background. Bullets: pure white, no outline.
   - Pixel art drawn 1× for 2× canvas (no subpixel or inconsistent pixel scale).
3. **Execution context:**
   - Does any "suspicious" container or curtain open? Confirm no "open" frame or state exists.

### How to Review a Line of Dialogue or Text
1. **Narrator conventions:**
   - Does narrative text start with `* `?
   - Is it written in second person ("You")?
   - Max 3 lines per dialogue box page with proper hanging indent?
2. **Content scan:**
   - Search for forbidden terms: check against `my child` (must be `pup`/`hun` for Furiell), `Pepe` (must be `Grub`), `FURRY OVER` (must be `WELCOME HOME` or `GAMER OVER`), `raid` (must be `squad` / Fortnite match).
   - Are there any minor-associated words or coded abuse terms? (Must be: ZERO).
   - Are real people or streamers mentioned? (Must be: ZERO).
   - If referencing an adult joke or "Homework" folder, is it handled via implication, cut-off, or refusal rather than graphic description?
3. **Pacing and Density:**
   - Does the humor respect the ~70/30 furry/gamer ratio?
   - Do game references avoid piling up and follow the pre-kill / post-kill rules?
   - Is English spelling and grammar clean, native, and without accents/untranslated words?
